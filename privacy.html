Privacy policy

How this demonstration portal handles information.

Demonstration system

The Village of Riverbend is fictitious. Every resident, property, and tax figure in this system is synthetic. Do not enter real personal information.

What we collect

Accounts store a username, a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hash, a display name, and an optional email address. Resident and property records in this demonstration are synthetic and generated locally.

How information is protected

  • Encryption at rest: contact details and assessment breakdowns are stored as AES-256-GCM ciphertext with a unique nonce per record.
  • Encryption in transit: when TLS is enabled, all traffic is encrypted. Security headers (HSTS, CSP, frame denial) are applied to every response.
  • Access control: role-based permissions with signed, expiring session tokens. Privileged record views are audited.
  • Tamper evidence: audit entries are chained with SHA-256 hashes and can be verified at /api/audit/verify.

What we do not do

  • No cookies are used for advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • No information is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics or third-party scripts are loaded on this site.

Disclaimer

This system is a software demonstration only. It is not a production municipal system, a security certification, or a compliance claim. Demonstration credentials are published intentionally. Real deployments require key management, identity proofing, governance, accessibility testing, backups, monitoring, and legal/privacy review.
