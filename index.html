How we protect your information

🔒 Encrypted session. Transport security (TLS), AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, and tamper-evident audit logging are active on this system.

Encryption at rest

Personal and assessment data is stored as AES-256-GCM ciphertext with a unique nonce per record and tamper-evident tags.

Encryption in transit

TLS-secured connections with HSTS, strict content security policy, frame denial, and clickjacking protection on every response.

Account security

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing (60,000 iterations), brute-force lockout, signed session tokens, and CSRF guards on all writes.
