Accessibility statement

This portal is built to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA, consistent with Government of Athabasca digital design standards.

Features

  • Skip links to main content and navigation on every page.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks, breadcrumbs, and labelled form fields.
  • Keyboard-operable controls with visible focus indicators (gold outline, per provincial brand standards).
  • Colour contrast meeting WCAG AA thresholds for text and interactive elements.
  • Responsive layouts supporting browser zoom up to 200%.
  • Print-friendly styles for council and office packages.

Known limitations

  • Large data tables scroll horizontally on small screens.
  • This demonstration has not completed a formal third-party audit.

Feedback

In a production deployment, accessibility feedback would be routed to the municipal office with a committed response window. For this demonstration, please report issues to the operator of this instance.
